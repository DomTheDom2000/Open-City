In Free Roam and Uber you can swap the car for the Streak motorbike in the garage: it is much faster, with its own upgrades and cosmetics. The Tractor has the Delivery Truck's speed and boost, with its own colours and performance upgrades, and keeps full speed on dirt, grass and fields. Weathered, half-collapsed houses in the old towns come down if you crash into them. Explore green pharmacies, hospital campuses, glass terminals, roof gardens and four distinct old-town styles. Airports range from regional airfields to international hubs with twin runways and larger airliners. Traffic includes hatchbacks, vans, trucks, buses, ambulances, fire trucks and motorbikes. Traffic drives at different speeds. Highway speeders and police drive aggressively. Emergency vehicles and highway speeders get priority at crossings. Ambulances and fire trucks keep normal following distances and cruise slightly faster than regular traffic. Fast motorcycle groups sometimes ride the expressways at night. Buildings and vehicles are solid. Ease off before a corner, or hold the handbrake to slide. Grass slows you down. Dirt trails have looser grip and a lower top speed. Open the map and use Find nearby to pin a residential, commercial or industrial sector, airport, giant Old Town, highway or giant reserve. Drive to earn spendable kilometres. Open the garage to upgrade the vehicle you are currently driving. Turbo recharges after use. While actively boosting, hitting another car makes it explode and clears it from your path—including parked cars and police. Every pursuing police car you destroy awards 100 bonus kilometres to spend in the garage. Glowing pickups appear occasionally as you drive: speed boost (15s), ghost drive through every obstacle with full speed on grass (18s), and infinite nitro (25s; boost even without a turbo upgrade). Collect them by driving over them. Ghost drive passes through buildings, traffic, police, planes, rocks and water. Cars you pass through while ghosting stay intact, even with nitro active. If ghost drive ends while you overlap an obstacle or vehicle, you can move clear before colliding with that object again. In Free Roam, turn on Police chase in the admin panel (`) for up to five coordinated pursuers: a chaser, an interceptor, two flankers and a unit aiming for junctions ahead. They close gaps at high speed, ram your car and intercept your path from ahead and both sides. Lost or destroyed units are quickly replaced ahead, outside your view. Changing mode ends the chase. Uber, bus and truck jobs have no time limit. Uber rides use your Runabout. Stop at the marked curb to pick up one passenger, then drive them to their drop-off. Bus jobs have 2–6 stops, including the first pickup and final drop-off. Each offer shows its total stops and bonus KM; the active job shows which stop you are on. Choose Accept job to take an offer. Every new assignment needs your acceptance. Routes range from regional trips to cross-country journeys. Follow the marked bays and stop for two seconds to load or unload. Completed drop-offs earn KM. The clean-driving bonus starts at 100% and drops by 5 points per collision with a vehicle or building. Cars destroyed with boost don’t count. At the final drop-off, the remaining percentage of the full job fare is paid as an extra bonus. Jobs, passengers and cargo wait for you when you switch modes, and are saved between sessions. Use Next job to get an offer with a pickup near your current location, even after roaming far away. You still need to accept it. Use End job to leave an unfinished assignment. The bright purple map route favours main roads, highways and longer straight stretches, avoiding old-town and airport shortcuts where practical. Short purple arrows on the road mark upcoming turns during accepted jobs. They appear earlier at speed, fade after each turn and leave long straight roads clear. Car, Bus and Truck keep separate performance and cosmetic builds. Free Roam and Uber share the Car garage. Train mode places your three-car train on the nearest clear railway, with matching outward-facing cabs at each end and a carriage in the middle. Use W / Up to go forward, S / Down to brake then reverse, and Space for stronger braking. Speed, acceleration and boost are equal in both directions. Between jobs, tap A / Left or D / Right to move one step through Left, Straight and Right for the next junction. For example, after choosing Right, tap Left once for Straight and again for Left. Choices follow your direction of travel, including while reversing. Without a selection the train continues straight, or follows your chosen map destination. You can change the queued turn until your leading cab enters the junction. During an accepted station job, junctions follow the job automatically. Stop with the center of any of the three cars inside the purple station marker for two seconds to load or unload. Railway jobs alternate passenger services and freight deliveries, with no time limit. The Train garage has its own upgrades. Open the map to set a rail destination between jobs. Occasional NPC trains stop at stations and obey shared signals. The entire railway, including junctions and stations, is elevated on continuous bridges. Road vehicles pass beneath it; trains and ground traffic never collide with or yield to each other. Tracks and bridge decks fade while driving a road vehicle and become fully opaque in Train mode. Switching back places your road vehicle on a nearby clear road. Your rail jobs, train position and upgrades are saved.
